Resume Matcher

Many companies filter resumes by keyword before a person reads them. Paste your resume and a job posting to see which skills the posting asks for that your resume never mentions.

Your text is scored in memory and is not stored or logged.

Tip: Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on Mac) also runs the check. PDF and Word files: copy the text out first.
